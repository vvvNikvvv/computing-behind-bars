What the categories mean

Degree pathway
Computing coursework that counts toward a degree. "Credential field" says whether the degree itself is in computing; a computing course inside a liberal arts degree is not a computer science degree.
Certificate
A structured, multi-course certificate program in a computing area.
Standalone course
One or more individual computing courses that are not part of a stated credential.
Other computing education
Digital literacy, IT training, or related instruction.

A missing marker does not mean no program exists, only that we have not documented one. See methodology.
